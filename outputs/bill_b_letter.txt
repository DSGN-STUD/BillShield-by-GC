SECTION 2 — STRUCTURAL VIOLATIONS: IMPERMISSIBLE UNBUNDLED CHARGES

Under CGHS OM 03.10.2025, Annexure-III §1 and §2, room and ward charges are explicitly defined as all-inclusive and comprehensive. The following charges are expressly subsumed within room/ward charges and may not be separately itemized or billed under any circumstances:

- MEDICAL RECORD CHARGES: ₹750
- ADMISSION CHARGES: ₹2,000
- BIOMEDICAL WASTE MANAGEMENT SERVICE: ₹200
- DOCUMENT PROCESSING CHARGES: ₹400
- CONSULTATION — DIETITIAN: ₹500
- NURSING CHARGE (three instances): ₹6,500 + ₹4,500 + ₹4,500 = ₹15,500

Total impermissibly unbundled charges: ₹19,350.

By separately billing these items, the hospital has effectively double-charged the patient for services that are already compensated within the room/ward rate. We require that all of the above line items be deleted in their entirety from the final bill and any settled or pending insurance claim.
