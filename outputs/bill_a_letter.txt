SECTION 1 — STRUCTURAL / UNBUNDLING VIOLATION

The following charge has been identified as a structural violation of the CGHS all-inclusive package framework under CGHS Office Memorandum dated 03.10.2025, Code OP100, and CGHS Annexure-III §1/§2:

Item: Hydrophobic Foldable IOL
Amount Billed Separately: ₹5,800.00
CGHS Code: OP100-NOTE
Applicable CGHS Package Rate (Tier I, Super-Speciality): ₹17,000 × 1.15 = ₹19,550.00 (all-inclusive, per eye)

The CGHS Code OP100 package for Phacoemulsification with Foldable IOL is explicitly designated as an ALL-INCLUSIVE rate. This means that the cost of the intraocular lens — including the Hydrophobic Foldable IOL — is subsumed within the package ceiling and may not be billed as a separate, additional line item outside that package. Billing the IOL independently alongside the procedure package (billed at ₹12,398 under CGHS Code No. 156) constitutes an unbundling of a component that CGHS policy expressly requires to be bundled. This is irrespective of whether the combined total of both line items (₹12,398 + ₹5,800 = ₹18,198) falls below the package ceiling of ₹19,550. The structural integrity of the package framework does not permit disaggregation of its constituent components under any circumstance.

The charge of ₹5,800.00 billed separately for the Hydrophobic Foldable IOL is therefore not payable as a standalone item and must be withdrawn from the bill entirely.
